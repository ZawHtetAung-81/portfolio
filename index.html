About

Marketing that closes the loop

I'm a Bachelor of International Business graduate from Rangsit International College (GPA 3.79), currently working as the primary English-language voice for JNN Global — a Bangkok-based commercial real estate and business services consultancy with operations in Thailand and China.

My work sits where marketing meets business development. I write and structure the content, but I also build the databases and follow-up processes that turn that content into an actual pipeline. That combination — narrative on one side, data and systems on the other — is what I bring to a team.

I'm looking for roles in sales and marketing, business development, or brand strategy, particularly in bilingual or cross-border contexts where working across English, Burmese, and Mandarin is an asset rather than a footnote.
